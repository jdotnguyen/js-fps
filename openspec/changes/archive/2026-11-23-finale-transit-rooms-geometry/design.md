# Design

## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md) for motivation and the three-phase rollout strategy.

Thalassa's `slice01` level is generated by `scripts/gen-slice01.mjs` into `src/data/levels/slice01.json` on a strict `4.0 m x 4.0 m` modular grid (`docs/modular-grid-standard.md`). Rooms define integer cell sets, ceiling heights (`3.2–5.0+ m`), optional sunken floors (`floorY` in `[-3, 0]`, `src/data/types/level.ts`), per-area palettes (`src/world/kit/palettes.ts`), doors, open seams (`opening(a, b, x, z, side)`), and kit props/structures (`src/world/kit/`). Visibility uses portal culling (`room-visibility` spec), and exterior ocean rendering activates only when a visible room includes `wall_window` tiles (`exterior-ocean` spec).

Currently:
- `R11` ("Control Room") is `3x3` cells (`X=8..10, Z=9..11`, height `4.0 m`) with 11 sparse props and 6 enemy spawners in group `r11_climax`.
- `R13d` ("Maintenance Corridor") is `3x1` cells (`X=8..10, Z=8`, height `3.2 m`), tagged `area: 'dive_bay'`, connected west to `R13b` via permanently sealed `D_R13b_R13d`, south to `R11` via `D_R13d_R11`, and blocked along its south wall (`z = 33.4`) by a 0.5 m `pipe_run`, `pipe_labels`, and `pipe_leak`.
- Grid cells east of `R13d`/`R11` (`X >= 11, Z >= 8`) are unoccupied (`R12d` Nutrient Room stops at `Z=7`).

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Deliver clean, walkable greybox/structural geometry for the entire lower transit path (`R11 -> R13d -> R15a -> Train Car A` and isolated `Train Car B -> R16a -> R16b -> R16c`) strictly on the `4.0 m` grid.
- Nail the architectural scale and movement choreography: `5.0 m` Power Bay -> compressed `3.2 m` Maintenance Corridor -> `5.0 m` North-South Departure Bay -> tight Train Car -> `~3.5–4.0 m` west exit platform -> left turn up `+0.6 m` wide stairs -> soaring `20 m x 24 m x 8.0 m` bank-entrance Arrival Concourse with symmetrical pillar rows and flanking check-in booths.
- Ensure every new room and greybox train shell passes headless collision, nav, wall-clearance, sunken-floor, and portal-culling tests, and is immediately inspectable via `?debug&jump=r11`, `?debug&jump=r15a`, and `?debug&jump=r16`.
- Coordinate implementation through the `threejs-game-director` skill (using `threejs-gameplay-systems` for spatial/collision/feel verification and `threejs-qa-release` for browser visual & build verification).

**Non-Goals:**
- Hero prop dressing (generators/turbines, switchgear cabinets, rail signs, blood decals, massacre props) — deferred to Phase 2.
- Animated scrolling abyssal tunnel ride, door-close transit state machine, or teleport handoff between Train Car A and Train Car B — deferred to Phase 2c.
- Rewriting `action_climax.json` with Giles power-up dialogue, Heins radio exchange, removing Giles's infection arc, new VO/music, or importing the new creature GLB — deferred to Phase 3.

## Decisions

### D1: Grid Coordinates & Room Topology (Pattern A: Twin Docked Cars)

We place the Departure Boarding Bay (`R15a`) directly east of `R13d` and isolate the Arrival Terminal (`R16a` + `R16b` + `R16c`) at detached grid coordinates (`X=20..24`, well clear of all existing rooms which end at `X=13`):

```text
DEPARTURE WING (attached to R10/R11)             ARRIVAL TERMINAL (isolated, X=20..24)

  X: 8   9  10 | 11   12   13                      X: 20   21   22   23   24
Z=8 [R13d---]=+=[R15a R15a R15a]  h=5.0m         Z=8   .  [R16a R16a R16a]  .   h=5.0m, floorY=-0.6
      ||       | Plat Trk  Win                   Z=9   .  [R16a TRN  R16a]  .   (center track + car)
Z=9 [R11  R11]| [R15a TRN  R15a]                 Z=10  .  [R16a STRS R16a]  .   (wide stairs +0.6m)
Z=10[R11  R11]| [R15a TRN  R15a]                 ========= OPEN SEAM (X=21..23) =========
Z=11[R11  R11]| [R15a R15a R15a]                 Z=11 [R16b R16b R16b R16b R16b] h=8.0m, y=0
  h=5.0m                                         Z=12 [R16b  [P]  ..  [P]  R16b] (pillar pair 1)
  (D_R10_R11 at 8,12,'n')                        Z=13 [R16b  [P]  ..  [P]  R16b] (pillar pair 2)
                                                 Z=14 [R16b  [P]  ..  [P]  R16b] (pillar pair 3)
                                                 Z=15 [R16b Bth   ..  Bth  R16b] (check-in booths)
                                                 Z=16 [R16b Bth   ..  Bth  R16b] (check-in booths)
                                                                  || D_R16b_R16c
                                                 Z=17  .  [R16c R16c R16c]  .   h=3.6m (Dispatch)
```

- **Why twin docked cars + isolated arrival terminal (Pattern A) over a 100 m physical track**: Keeps both train shells stationary for crisp collision/NPC standing, lets Phase 2c scroll an exterior abyssal tunnel rig past the windows for arbitrary dialogue duration, and guarantees `room-visibility` portal culling never draws the Arrival Terminal from the main station.
- **Why southbound train travel**: Exiting the train's **west (right-side)** doors in `R16a` places Jay on cell `X=21` facing west (`~3.75 m` from car door to the west outer wall); turning **left (south)** faces straight up the wide stairs across the south edge of `R16a` into `R16b`. The exact same west-side doors face the west boarding platform (`X=11`) in `R15a`.

### D2: Sunken Arrival Dock (`floorY: -0.6`) + Deck-Level Concourse (`y = 0`)

- **Decision**: Author `R16a` with `floorY: -0.6` (using the engine's existing sunken-floor support from `level-kit`) and `R16b` at deck level `y = 0` (`height: 8.0`). Join cells `(21,10)`, `(22,10)`, `(23,10)` of `R16a` to `(21,11)`, `(22,11)`, `(23,11)` of `R16b` via `opening()`. Place wide `stairs` (`rise: 0.6, run: 1.8, width: 3.8, rails: 'none'`) along the south edge of `R16a` cells `(21,10)` and `(23,10)` (and/or across `(22,10)` in front of the train buffer stop) so walking south from the west exit platform climbs straight up 3–4 broad steps onto `R16b`.
- **Rationale**: `src/data/types/level.ts` already validates `floorY: optional(numRange(-3, 0))` and `level.ts` / `sunkenFloor.test.ts` already handle walls reaching down to `floorY` and walkable stairs climbing to `y = 0`. Using `floorY: -0.6` avoids adding positive-floor-height machinery to the level schema.
- **Seam check**: Because `R16a` (`height: 5.0`, `floorY: -0.6`) opens into `R16b` (`height: 8.0`, `y = 0`), we need a solid header/fascia slab closing the vertical step between `y = 5.0` and `y = 8.0` on `R16b`'s north seam over `X=21..23`, plus clean riser backing between `y = -0.6` and `y = 0` under the stairs so no void leaks at the height transitions.

### D3: Structural Greybox Pieces in `src/world/kit/transit.ts`

Create a dedicated, minimal kit module `src/world/kit/transit.ts` registered in `KitRegistry` (`src/world/registry.ts`) with clean procedural greybox pieces (real colliders, `SURF_metal`/`SURF_concrete` floor tags, nav nodes, zero z-fighting):
1. **`transit_train_shell`**: Walkable ~`3.0 m` wide x `10.0 m` long x `2.8 m` tall rail car shell centered on the track cell column, with solid floor (`y = 0` relative to room floor, flush with platform or single `0.12 m` step), solid front/back bulkheads, east & west side walls with large transparent window openings, and a `1.6 m`-wide open west boarding doorway (leaves open in Phase 1 greybox so the player can walk straight in/out). Includes roof slab and interior floor/wall colliders.
2. **`concourse_pillar`**: Tall architectural column (`~1.2 m x 1.2 m` square with chamfered base/capital, parameterized `height`, default `8.0 m`) wide enough to visually conceal a large creature behind it from the stair approach while keeping wide `>= 3 m` flanking clearance.
3. **`checkin_booth`**: Modular bank/customs-style check-in booth enclosure (`~3.6 m` wide x `2.4 m` deep x `3.2 m` tall) with a waist-high counter, side partitions, upper fascia, and open walk-behind/walk-past clearance along the central concourse aisle.
4. **`track_bed`**: Shallow sunken/recessed twin-rail track slab (`3.8 m` wide x `4.0 m` long) marking the North-South rail line under the train and extending toward the north/south tunnel mouths.
5. **`seam_header`**: Simple structural bulkhead fascia box closing height steps where a `5.0 m` room (`R16a`) opens seamlessly into an `8.0 m` room (`R16b`), preventing any void gap above the opening.

### D4: Area Palette & Room Detachment

- Add a `transit` area palette in `src/world/kit/palettes.ts` (clean industrial slate/concrete floor, brushed steel trim, dark ceiling, clean hazard striping) for `R11`, `R13d`, `R15a`, `R16a`, `R16b`, `R16c`.
- Keep `R13d`'s existing `rustBlotch`/`diamondRusty` overrides in Phase 1 so the `surface-textures` spec's `R13d` scenario remains green until Phase 2b revisits `R13d`'s surface finish.
- Keep `area: 'control'` (or map `R11` to `control` / `transit`) — wait: check `slice01.test.ts` line 73 which asserts `'control'` is among the room areas! Keeping `R11` on `area: 'control'` (or updating that single area list in `slice01.test.ts` to `'transit'`) — updating `R11` + `R13d` + `R15a` + `R16*` to `area: 'transit'` is cleaner long-term, and we explicitly update `slice01.test.ts`'s area assertion in `tasks.md`.

### D5: Minimal Sequence & Test Compatibility in Phase 1

- Removing `D_R13b_R13d` and `r11_climax` spawns in Phase 1 touches existing sequences/tests that expected them:
  - Remove `D_R13b_R13d` `doorState` steps from `src/data/sequences/action_climax.json` and `src/data/sequences/action_restore.json`.
  - Remove `spawnGroup` / `waitGroup` for `r11_climax` from `action_climax.json` (so `R11` has zero combat immediately, while deferring the full Power Bay + Train + Heins + Monster script rewrite to Phase 3).
  - Remove `dive_leak` sequence from `src/data/sequences/rounds.json` and `dive_leak_cool` from `quiet_restore.json` (since the blocking `R13d` pipe and leak are removed).
  - Update `slice01.test.ts`, `gather.test.ts`, `action.test.ts`, and `quiet.test.ts` to match the new geometry and removed Dive Bay door.
  - Keep `R11` anchors needed by the still-present Phase 3 placeholder script (`giles_final`, `final_console`, `r11_hold`, `r11_giles_task`, `r11_okafor_end`, `r11_okafor_step`, `r11_jay_end`) intact until Phase 3 replaces them.

### D6: Execution via `threejs-game-director`

During `openspec-apply-change`, implementation will follow the `threejs-game-director` skill workflow:
- Route geometry & kit-piece construction through `threejs-gameplay-systems` / Thalassa's `modular-grid-standard.md` rules.
- Visually inspect every room (`?debug&jump=r11`, `r15a`, `r16`) in the browser and verify sightlines, proportions, lighting legibility, stair climb, and zero seam/void leaks before marking Phase 1 complete.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[Height mismatch void at `R16a` (`5.0 m`) -> `R16b` (`8.0 m`) open seam]** → Mitigation: Place explicit `seam_header` geometry across `X=21..23` from `y = 5.0` to `y = 8.0` and verify from both sides in-browser and via raycast/collider checks.
- **[Sunken-floor anchor validation in `R16a` (`floorY: -0.6`)]** → Mitigation: Place all `R16a` anchors and train shell pieces at `y = -0.6` (matching `sunkenPlacementProblems` in `src/world/level.ts`) and keep `R16b` anchors at `y = 0`.
- **[Ceiling height `8.0 m` in `R16b` with existing `wall_plain`/`wall_panel` tiles]** → Mitigation: Verify `wallTile` in `src/world/kit/structure.ts` scales cleanly to `height: 8.0` (backing, rails, trim, and wall colliders) without floating bands or UV stretching; adjust tile vertical tiling if needed.
- **[Lighting attenuation in an `8.0 m`-tall `20 m x 24 m` hall]** → Mitigation: Give `R16b` neutral greybox ceiling/fill lights with sufficient range (`16–20 m`) in Phase 1 so proportions, pillars, and booth alcoves read clearly during walk-through (flickering horror lighting comes in Phase 2d).
- **[Exterior ocean draw cost in `R15a`]** → Mitigation: `R15a`'s east `wall_window` tiles only trigger exterior ocean drawing when `R15a` is visible (`exterior-ocean` spec), adding zero cost anywhere else in the station.
