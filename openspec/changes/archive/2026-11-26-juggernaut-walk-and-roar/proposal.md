# Proposal

## Why

The R16 finale's Juggernaut glides 3.4 m out from behind the pillar as a statue. Its second roar plays over a black screen. With the creature rig in place (`rig-juggernaut`), it can walk out on CAM 10 and act both roars, so the cliffhanger lands on the creature itself, not on a sound in the dark. Its walk is also the first piece of the motion the later fights need.

## What Changes

- **Reusable creature gait.** It walks a creature along a path at a given speed:
  - steps in a diagonal-sequence knuckle-walk (hind leg, opposite hand, other hind leg, other hand);
  - plants each hand and foot with the rig's limb IK, and lifts it on an arc between plants;
  - bobs and sways the body with its steps;
  - starts from standing and settles back onto all four limbs when it stops;
  - reports each touchdown, so the game can play a footfall there.
- **Timed key-pose sequences.** A creature's pose data can hold sequences of named poses, each with a blend time, a hold, jaw and tongue amounts, and a tremble. A player steps through them while the gait carries on underneath. The Juggernaut gains two:
  - **a walking roar**, upper body only;
  - **a full roar:** wind up, rear back, jaw wide, trembling hold, settle.
- **CAM 10 finale.**
  1. The Juggernaut walks the same 3.4 m from behind the pillar (`r16_monster_hide` to `r16_monster_reveal`) over about the same time, facing Giles and Okafor.
  2. It acts the CCTV roar as it walks.
  3. It stops and acts the long roar (`r16MonsterRoarBlackout`) on camera. Giles and Okafor keep firing throughout, their lights on its face. Under the long roar their gunfire is quieter, so the roar overpowers it, and each screams "What the hell is that?!" over the radio.
  4. On the roar's last beat the scene cuts hard to black and dead silence.
  5. About a second later, `story:cliffhanger` brings up the THALASSA card.
  
  The roar in the dark is removed.
- **Footfalls.** New heavy creature footfalls (feet) and knuckle thuds (hands), generated with ElevenLabs through the footsteps pipeline and played on each touchdown.

**Not in this change:** turning on the spot, breathing and flesh springs, the other set pieces (swipe, slam, peek, lunge), and anything in the fights.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `creature-motion`: how a rigged creature moves: its gait along a path with planted limbs and touchdown events, and timed key-pose sequences (roars and other set pieces) layered over the gait.

### Modified Capabilities
- `transit-wing`: the CAM 10 reveal walks the Juggernaut out and acts both roars on camera, then cuts to black and silence before the THALASSA card. The roar in the dark is removed.

## Impact

- **Code:** `src/actors/creature/` gains the gait and the sequence player. `src/data/types/creatures.ts` gains pose sequences, and `src/data/creatures/juggernaut.*.json` gains the roar poses and sequences. `src/render/fx/juggernaut.ts` walks and roars the creature. `src/app/session.ts` wires footfalls and roar events.
- **Data:** `src/data/sequences/action_climax.json` gets the CAM 10 beats.
- **Audio:** new clips in `public/assets/audio/footsteps/`, generated by `scripts/footsteps-generate.ts` and registered in `src/audio/sounds.ts`.
- **Sandbox:** the creature sandbox gains walk and sequence controls.
- **Tests:** gait, sequence player and finale tests, and `src/script/action.test.ts`.
- **Depends on `rig-juggernaut`.** That change is implemented but uncommitted. Its full test run is still owed (task 8.1).
