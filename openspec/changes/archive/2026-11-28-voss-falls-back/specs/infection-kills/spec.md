read as coming out of her back. She falls back from it instead.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Voss falls back from the creature
After the burst, Voss SHALL recoil straight back from the glass and fall backward to the floor, face up, her head away
from the pane, so the creature that came out of the front of her torso is left standing in the space she gives up. She
SHALL end clear of the spot the creature stands on, and a restored or skipped kill SHALL leave her in the same place.

#### Scenario: Falls back from the glass
- **WHEN** the burst and still beats play
- **THEN** her hips move only away from the pane, more than 1 m in all, no joint of hers crosses the pane, and no part of
  her passes through the bench, the gurney or the cart
- **AND** she comes to rest on the floor face up, her head at least 0.3 m farther from the pane than her hips

#### Scenario: Out of her front
- **WHEN** the creature first appears in the burst
- **THEN** its head is in line with her chest, at her chest's height, and on the pane's side of her chest
- **AND** her chest never comes back in front of its head toward the pane, and by the end of the burst the creature's head
  is at least 0.4 m in front of her chest

#### Scenario: Room for the creature
- **WHEN** the creature stands to pound on the glass after the kill
- **THEN** no part of Voss's body is within 0.3 m of its feet

#### Scenario: Restored in place
- **WHEN** the drop is restored from a checkpoint taken after the kill
- **THEN** Voss lies where a played kill leaves her, her hips within 5 cm of that spot
