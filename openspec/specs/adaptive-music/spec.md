# adaptive-music Specification

## Purpose

Defines scored set pieces during play: music whose layers follow game events and the player's position, stays in time
on one shared clock, and gets out of the way of the sounds that matter.

## Requirements

### Requirement: A score is a set of layers on one clock
A score SHALL be made of layers that loop together on one shared musical clock, plus one-shot stingers. All layers SHALL
start at the same moment and stay in step for as long as the score plays. The score SHALL play on the `music` bus.

#### Scenario: Layers stay in step
- **WHEN** the Lab B score has played for 3 minutes
- **THEN** every layer is still at the same position in its loop as every other layer

#### Scenario: Music volume
- **WHEN** the player sets music volume to zero while the score plays
- **THEN** the score is silent and the rest of the mix is unchanged

### Requirement: Cues change layer levels
A score SHALL change its layer levels when a cue arrives, fading each layer to that cue's level. A cue SHALL arrive from
a game event the score names or from a sequence step. A cue SHALL be able to cut every layer to silence at once with no
fade.

#### Scenario: Beat cue
- **WHEN** the Lab B kill's panic beat begins
- **THEN** the pulse and the rising strings fade in within about one second

#### Scenario: Hard cut at the burst
- **WHEN** the burst beat begins
- **THEN** the score is silent within 50 ms and stays silent until the still beat

### Requirement: Stingers play on cue
A score SHALL play a named stinger when a cue asks for it, at once and over the layers. An unknown stinger name SHALL log
one warning and play nothing.

#### Scenario: Stab on a palm hit
- **WHEN** Voss's palm hits the Lab B glass
- **THEN** a string stab plays at the same moment

### Requirement: The score exposes its beat
While a score plays, other systems SHALL be able to ask for the time of its next beat. With no score playing, the next
beat SHALL be now.

#### Scenario: No score
- **WHEN** a step waits for the next beat and no score is playing
- **THEN** it runs at once

### Requirement: Distance drives layers
A score SHALL be able to tie a layer's level to the player's distance from a target, between a far level and a near
level, while a cue has that tie turned on.

#### Scenario: Walking towards a target
- **WHEN** a score has a layer tied to the distance from a door and the player walks towards that door
- **THEN** that layer grows louder as the distance shrinks

### Requirement: The score makes room for in-world hits
A score SHALL be able to dip its level briefly when a named game event fires, and recover within about a second.

#### Scenario: A hit the score names
- **WHEN** a score ducks on an event and that event fires
- **THEN** the score dips by the score's set amount and is back to its level within about a second

### Requirement: The score resumes from checkpoints
A score SHALL say from which checkpoint states it resumes and at which cue. Restoring to such a state SHALL start the
score at that cue. Restarting or returning to the title SHALL stop it.

#### Scenario: Death in the contact fight
- **WHEN** the player dies in the R06 fight and restores at `cp_contact_clear`
- **THEN** the Lab B score starts in its hold, without replaying the kill layers

#### Scenario: Restore after the escape
- **WHEN** the player restores at `cp_drop`
- **THEN** the Lab B score starts in its hold, and neither the escape nor its stinger plays again

#### Scenario: Back to the title
- **WHEN** the player quits to the title while the score plays
- **THEN** the score stops and the menu theme takes the music bus

### Requirement: The score is generated, and missing audio is safe
A score's layers and stingers SHALL be generated by the game, identical on every machine, and SHALL NOT block play while
they render. A cue that arrives before the score is ready SHALL be remembered, so the score starts at the latest cue
once it is ready.

#### Scenario: Drop before the render finishes
- **WHEN** the Lab B kill starts before the score has rendered
- **THEN** the kill plays without music and the score comes in at the current beat's cue once it is ready

### Requirement: The score frees its audio when done
A score SHALL release its rendered audio once the level no longer needs it.

#### Scenario: After the contact fight
- **WHEN** the cover-me scene is done
- **THEN** the Lab B score's audio is released

### Requirement: The Lab B score follows the incident
The Lab B score SHALL change its layers with each kill beat, from a drone and heartbeat at scratch to full percussion at
the arch, cut to silence at the burst, drop to the drone in the escape's blackout, hold from the escape through R05,
bring the full layers back when the creature drops into R06, and end with a final hit when the creature is killed.

#### Scenario: Layers follow the beats
- **WHEN** the kill moves from scratch to the arch
- **THEN** more layers play at each beat, and the percussion layer plays only from the arch

#### Scenario: Quiet in the dark
- **WHEN** Lab B's lights die in the escape
- **THEN** only the drone plays until the lights come back

### Requirement: The Lab B score holds a steady level
Apart from the burst's silence, the escape's blackout and its stingers, the Lab B score SHALL play at one loudness from
scratch until the creature is killed, about as loud as the menu theme or louder. It SHALL NOT follow the player's distance
or dip for the creature's blows. The room ambience SHALL step back while it plays.

#### Scenario: Same loudness at every beat
- **WHEN** the score's loudness is measured at each cue that plays layers
- **THEN** every cue is within 1 dB of the others

#### Scenario: Ambience under the score
- **WHEN** the Lab B score is playing
- **THEN** the room ambience is about 6 dB lower than without it, and back to normal once the score ends

#### Scenario: Ends when the creature dies
- **WHEN** the player kills the creature in R06
- **THEN** a final hit plays and the drone fades within about 3 s

#### Scenario: Door slam
- **WHEN** the creature bursts through the R06 vent grate
- **THEN** a slam stinger plays and the fight layers come back

### Requirement: The medbay score follows the scene
The medbay score SHALL start when the security office scene starts and SHALL change its layers with each section. It SHALL
carry a warm motif for Priya through the rescue, cut to silence apart from the medical bay monitor at the surgery's fall
and at the creature's stare, build through the walk and the kill, and end when the intercom is switched off.

#### Scenario: Her motif
- **WHEN** the triage and the armory sections play
- **THEN** the Priya motif layer plays, and it does not play during the kill

#### Scenario: Silence at the fall
- **WHEN** the tray falls in the surgery
- **THEN** every score layer drops out within 0.2 s for about 2 s, and the monitor beep is still heard

#### Scenario: Silence at the stare
- **WHEN** the creature stops and stares at the player
- **THEN** every score layer drops out for the stare's hold, and the monitor beep is still heard

#### Scenario: Ends with the intercom
- **WHEN** Okafor switches the intercom off in the aftermath
- **THEN** the medbay score has ended, with no layer still playing

### Requirement: The medbay score keeps the monitor's time
The medbay score's beat SHALL match the interval of the medical bay's vitals monitor beep, and the monitor SHALL keep that
steady interval through the scene while the score builds over it.

#### Scenario: One clock
- **WHEN** the medbay score plays during the rescue
- **THEN** the score's beat interval equals the monitor beep's interval to within 10 ms

#### Scenario: The monitor does not race
- **WHEN** the score builds during the kill
- **THEN** the monitor beep's interval is unchanged

### Requirement: Distance to the glass drives the glass layer
The medbay score's glass layer SHALL grow louder as the player gets closer to the medical bay panes and quieter as he
moves away, within the security office.

#### Scenario: Walking up to the glass
- **WHEN** the player walks from the back of the security office to the viewing strip during the rescue
- **THEN** the glass layer is louder at the strip than at the back of the room

### Requirement: Her motif returns in the aftermath
In the aftermath, the Priya motif SHALL play once more, slower and detuned, under the silence, and SHALL end before
Okafor's first aftermath line.

#### Scenario: The last time
- **WHEN** the aftermath begins
- **THEN** the Priya motif plays slower and detuned, and has stopped before "I told her she was fine." starts

### Requirement: Bus events cue only live scores
A game event SHALL cue only a score that is loaded and has not been released or begun fading out. A game event SHALL NOT
load or restart a score. A sequence's score step SHALL load or start a score as before.

#### Scenario: A released score stays quiet
- **WHEN** Priya's kill reaches its still beat after the Lab B score was released in R06
- **THEN** the Lab B score does not play, and only the medbay score is heard

#### Scenario: Voss's kill still drives Lab B
- **WHEN** the drop loads the Lab B score and Voss's kill emits its beat events
- **THEN** the Lab B score follows each beat's cue as before

### Requirement: The Lab B escape ends on a jump stinger
When Lab B's lights come back on after the escape and show the room empty, a loud, sudden jump stinger SHALL play
at that moment, over the Lab B score's hold. World sound apart from the escape's own sounds SHALL be silenced in the dark
before it and come back with it.

#### Scenario: The reveal
- **WHEN** Lab B's lights come back on in the escape
- **THEN** the jump stinger starts within 0.1 s of the lights, and the room sounds come back with it

#### Scenario: Only the creature in the dark
- **WHEN** Lab B is dark during the escape
- **THEN** the only sounds are the vent cover tearing, the bangs moving away overhead, the squad's lines and the drone
