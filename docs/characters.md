# Character backgrounds (writer's bible)

Companion to ADR-0022. ADR-0022 holds the decisions; this file holds the depth behind them.
Status: Accepted (Justin confirmed Okafor's platform-fire past, Priya as the R08 victim, and the supporting-cast names) / Proposed (everything else). Fixed by earlier decisions and not reopened here: Giles hides the
wound for his contract and his daughter; Okafor never suspects him and steps toward him at the
reveal; the player is **Jay**, silent, with **no written backstory with anyone** in the squad.

## How to use this file
- **Iceberg rule.** Write the whole character here; show maybe a tenth of it in the slice. The
  player should feel the depth without being told it.
- **Every detail needs a way to surface:** a line, a prop, a behaviour or a place. Each character
  ends with a "Surfaces as" list. If a detail can never surface, it is only here to keep the
  writing consistent, and that is fine.
- **Truth vs. what people believe.** Where a character is wrong about something, both are written
  down, so lines can be wrong in a consistent way.

## August Giles, engineer

### Life before Thalassa
- Born and raised in a shipyard town; his father fitted ballast systems on cargo hulls. Giles
  learned pumps before he learned algebra and still thinks of every system as plumbing.
- Trade certificate at 19, offshore rigs at 22, first deep station at 27. Eleven years on deep
  stations since: always contract, never staff. Contract pays better, and he likes leaving.
- Married young, divorced at 33, amicably and tiredly. His ex-wife, Dana, raises their daughter
  **Mara** (9) topside. Giles is the parent who sends money and voice messages, not the one who
  shows up, and he knows it.
- Mara is in a good school he can only afford on deep-station hazard pay. One more two-year
  contract and her place is paid through to the end. This is the whole of why he stays quiet.

### At Thalassa (2 years)
- Systems engineer on a TAG maintenance contract: pressure, power, ventilation, coolant. Knows the
  station's guts better than anyone on staff, and staff treat him like the help.
- Sleeps badly in a bunk next to a coolant manifold that knocks at night (Okafor's running joke
  about how he looks comes from this).
- Thursday coffee with Okafor started when Okafor stitched his hand after a valve slipped in his
  first month. Giles made a joke through the whole procedure; Okafor didn't laugh once. They have
  been friends since.

### What he believes about TAG
- TAG is a landlord: it pays, it doesn't care, and it will drop you the moment you cost money.
  He has seen two contractors flown out "on medical" and never rehired. He is not paranoid; he
  is right.
- He doesn't know what Lab B does and has made a point of not asking.

### The three days (truth, for the writers)
| Day | What happened |
|---|---|
| -3 | Called to a coolant leak on the line that passes through Lab B's service wall. A lab-side access panel was warped from the inside; he cut his forearm on its edge. The coolant was warm, which it never is. Through the panel gap he saw something on the lab floor that looked like roots in a person's shape. He sealed the panel, logged "leak repaired" and said nothing. |
| -2 | The cut won't close properly and itches under the skin. He starts wearing long sleeves. Lab B goes into "routine quarantine". He understands what that means and decides it can't mean him. |
| -1 | Fingers twitch while he works. Short with people for the first time in two years. Calls Mara, talks about nothing for twenty minutes. |
| 0 (slice) | Arrives for the morning shift and does what he always does: makes it funny. |

### Personality in depth
- **Humour as load-bearing structure.** Jokes are how he keeps a situation from being serious.
  The more frightened he is, the more he jokes, which is why he is so funny in the slice.
- **Competence is his dignity.** He is never wrong about machines. When the bloom starts taking
  his hands, the first thing he loses is the one thing he was sure of.
- **Talks to machines, not to people, about feelings.** "Come on, sweetheart, hold for me" to a
  pressure door is the closest he gets to saying he's scared.
- **Small kindnesses, offhand.** Fixes the cook's broken kettle without being asked; holds doors.
- **Flaw:** he would rather carry something alone than be a burden. It looks like bravery; it is
  pride and fear in a trench coat.

### Habits and props
- A worn multitool on his belt, the grip wrapped in electrical tape.
- A photo of Mara taped inside the lid of his toolbox (seen when he opens it at a panel).
- Long sleeves, cuffs pulled down. Rubs his forearm when he thinks no one is looking.
- Drinks two coffees, always argues for a third.
- Hums tunelessly while working; the hum stops at infection stage 3.

### Surfaces as
- Intro: forearm rubbing, "slept on it wrong", coffee bargaining with Okafor.
- R06 panel: toolbox lid opens, the photo is visible for a second; "come on, sweetheart".
- R07: "cut it on the panel" (half true: it *was* a panel, three days ago).
- Late quiet moment: the one Mara line (e.g. "My kid thinks I fix submarines. Close enough.").
- Reveal: "Three days. The Lab B panel. I was going to say after the shift."

## Dr. Tomas Okafor, medic

### Life before Thalassa
- Grew up in a port city, eldest of four; his mother was a midwife. He learned early that calm is
  a tool, and that panic in the room belongs to everyone except the person treating.
- Trauma surgeon, then fifteen years on topside rescue flights: oil platforms, ship fires, divers
  with the bends. Very good at it.
- **The flight he doesn't talk about:** a platform fire, eleven casualties, four seats. He triaged a
  young welder with no visible injuries as walking wounded. The welder had internal bleeding and
  died on the deck while Okafor worked on someone else. Okafor did nothing wrong by the book. He
  stopped flying a year later.
- Took Thalassa because a station has one doctor, a small crew, and time to look at everyone
  properly. That is his penance and his comfort: here, nobody is seen too late.
- Widower; his wife Ngozi died of an illness six years ago. No children. He writes letters to his
  youngest sister, who sends back photos of her children he keeps in his desk.

### At Thalassa (4 years)
- Station medical officer: one surgery, one nurse (**Priya Anand**, see below), a sickbay of six beds.
- Has filed three requests to see Lab B's medical records under station health rules. All three
  came back "approved, pending". He has kept the replies.
- Thursday coffee with Giles; Giles is the only person on the station who makes him laugh, though
  he almost never lets it show.

### What he believes about TAG and Lab B
- He believes Lab B is running something biological without the containment it should have, and
  that "routine quarantine" is paperwork covering it. He is right.
- He has not connected it to Giles at all. Giles is his friend, Giles is a pipe man, and Giles
  says he slept on his arm. He believes him **completely** [Decided].

### Personality in depth
- **Calm as discipline, not temperament.** He is not naturally calm; he practises it. In the
  slice, the moments his voice drops are the moments he is most afraid.
- **Notices everything, accuses no one.** "It's not a diagnosis. It's an observation." He gives
  people the dignity of telling him themselves. That habit is exactly how he misses Giles.
- **Orders as care.** "Sit." "One coffee." "Eat something." Short commands are how he says he
  cares; he would be embarrassed to say it any other way.
- **Flaw:** trusts people to tell him the truth about their own bodies. With every stranger he is
  thorough; with his friend he took the joke.

### Habits and props
- A penlight in his breast pocket, clicks it when thinking.
- Reading glasses he pushes up onto his head and forgets.
- One coffee, black, always half-finished.
- A small notebook; writes down every patient's name, including the dead.

### Surfaces as
- Intro: "It's not a diagnosis. It's an observation." "Arm bothering you?"
- R05: "Legs! Take its legs!" — the surgeon finding the practical answer before anyone else.
- R08: watches Priya dragged away through the glass; says her name once, then works.
- Biometric door (R09): his specialist moment; he hesitates before badging into the med lab.
- Reveal: steps toward Giles ("August-"); his guilt is that he never once doubted him.

## The station and the company

### Thalassa Deep Research Station (placeholder name)
- ~3,000 m down, 60 crew at full complement, about 40 aboard during the slice.
- Officially: deep-sea mineral surveys and "marine biochemistry".
- Feels like: a ship that never sails. Warm, damp, everything humming, condensation on cold
  bulkheads, notices taped over notices.

### TAG Industries
- Retro-corporate, polite, unaccountable (styled like Weyland-Yutani; logo already in game).
- Runs Thalassa as a cost centre. Staff are TAG employees; Giles and the kitchen are contractors.
- Corporate voice in the slice: the PA, the quarantine notices, terminal messages. Always calm,
  always passive voice ("An incident has been contained.").

### Lab B and the bloom (truth, never fully stated in the slice)
- Lab B grew a deep-vent organism, a fungal-like colony that spreads through warm fluids and
  rewrites tissue toward its own growth. Researchers nicknamed it **the bloom** for how it opens.
- It got into the coolant loop that passes through Lab B's service wall about four days before
  the slice. Giles's cut was the first exposure outside the lab.
- TAG's response: quarantine Lab B, keep working, file it as routine. The lab team were already
  turning by the time of the slice.

### Outbreak timeline
| Day | Event |
|---|---|
| -10 | Lab B begins live culture work. |
| -4 | Containment breach into the coolant loop; first researcher symptomatic. |
| -3 | Giles cuts his arm on the Lab B panel. |
| -2 | "Routine quarantine" of Lab B announced. Okafor's third records request goes unanswered. |
| -1 | Lab B stops eating in the mess (the tech's line in the intro). |
| 0 | The slice: the drop in R04, lockdown, the long morning. |

## Relationships map
- **Giles ↔ Okafor:** close friends, two years, Thursday coffee. Giles makes him laugh; Okafor
  stitches him up. The reveal breaks this.
- **Okafor ↔ Priya:** mentor and nurse; she's the closest thing he has to family aboard. Her death
  in R08 is the moment his calm cracks for the first time.
- **Giles ↔ Bram:** two contractors; Giles fixes his kitchen things, Bram saves him the last pastry.
- **Okafor ↔ Hale/TAG:** three unanswered records requests. Polite, cold war.
- **Jay ↔ everyone:** colleagues on the station. Nothing more is written [Decided].

## Writing rules that follow from all this
1. Giles never says he is afraid. Okafor never says he cares. Show both through action.
2. Giles's jokes get faster through the slice and stop at stage 3; his humming stops with them.
3. Okafor says a person's name only when it matters (Priya once; "August" at the reveal).
4. TAG never uses active voice.
5. Nobody explains the bloom fully. Voss's log comes closest.
